# RT Buyback refresh policy

Updated 8 October 2026 after Shane requested current-price checks for every model on every refresh. This supersedes older 20/96-row samples, 1,439-row recent/demand filters, and fixed 12-batch research templates.

## Every refresh covers the complete catalogue

1. Read the current database and inline database, latest completed refresh and shipped pricing rules. Preserve unrelated work and prevent overlapping refreshes. Create a fresh dated work folder and baseline backups.
2. Run `_market_refresh_runner.py init --work <new-dated-folder>`. Its `all-catalogue-v1` manifest includes every domestic model and exact RAM/storage/size/connectivity variant. Do not filter by tier, popularity, launch age, discontinued status or a fixed count. Check new India launches and missing real variants too; add only with evidence and regenerate the manifest after an addition.
3. Research each variant's **current available new cash price** from fresh exact primary listings. Use official brand stores and multiple relevant retailers, including Flipkart and Reliance Digital. Search results only discover sources. Include mandatory fees and taxes; exclude conditional bank/exchange/EMI discounts, MRP-only references and unavailable stock. Verify exact variant, market, product condition and availability. Save source URL, actual read timestamp and hashed page evidence. Never use a higher seller merely to inflate buyback.
4. Phone collectors are helpers, not the entire workflow. Tablets, watches, laptops and audio require category-specific exact source checks for size, generation, connectivity and capacity. Collector gaps need a second source/manual research pass. Account separately for existing international-derived entries and their India sibling/regional rule; do not present an India price as verified international stock.
5. Every domestic key must have a result: `verified`, `unavailable` or `unverified`. Missing verified prices remain explicitly pending with a reason and `new_price_checks`, a list of `{source_url, observed_at, outcome}` documenting attempted sources. Do not delete a model or freshen old prices because current stock was not found. Save a per-model pending report. Never call partial verified-price coverage a full current-price feed.
6. Research used resale independently. Distinguish actual sold evidence from retailer asks and inferred estimates. Preserve source asks and assumptions. Current retailer-derived A1 estimates use a 10% retailer/service allowance for normal phones and 15% for C/D or foldables; no additional model-launch-age haircut. Actual device age comes only from the original purchase/bill date or supplied age. Keep the existing condition calculation, target margins, minimum resale margin, scaled 13% warranty and fresh assessed Cashify 2%/minimum Rs100 policy. Do not manufacture a competitor quote or used sale.
7. Candidate observations must carry `scope_policy: "all-catalogue-v1"`. Run `_market_refresh_runner.py coverage --work <folder> --observations <file>`; the writer also rejects missing/duplicate/unexpected models and undocumented pending checks under this policy. Review source/stock/fee mismatches, price outliers and storage inconsistencies. Use the current pricing engine; old scripts and hard-coded workflow batches are historical templates.
8. Validate the candidate and source evidence, run the seven pricing suites plus collector/scope regressions, confirm the baseline has not changed, and write `phone_db.json` and inline `const DB` together. Preserve per-price observation dates. Report total catalogue records, domestic variants checked, international derivations, verified new prices, used estimates, pending variants and actual quote changes.
9. Keep existing schedule, publication and notification settings. This policy changes research coverage, not those settings. Authorized deployment uses only the seven public runtime files and requires live readback. Distinguish local-ready and live-deployed states.

Setup verification on 8 October: 2,469 total records, 2,454 domestic variants (2,287 phones plus 167 other devices) and 15 international derivations. These counts must be recalculated each run. No new market refresh was executed as part of this schedule-policy change.

## Conservative quote resolution (31 October 2026)

Keep operational `quote_safety` limits separate from observed market prices. Pin unapproved rises above 8% to the preceding release, then reduce smaller-variant quick offers until comparable automatic A1 quotes have no RAM/storage inversion. Apply caps before condition, fold risk, warranty and selling-cost adjustments; never apply deductions twice. If a correction would exceed a 20% decrease, require confirmed current new cash price, achievable A1 resale and actual device age before quoting. These limits do not replace the fully verified exact-age formula. Never change source prices, timestamps or international records to make the ordering pass. Preserve pending comparisons separately from verified numeric ordering.

Run `test_quote_safety.cjs` and `test_quote_safety_solver.py` with the existing regression suites. Build publications with `_build_public.cjs`; only its seven runtime files may be deployed.
