 Frequently Asked Questions 
 Common questions regarding repairs, screen replacements, testing, warranties, and visiting our Gaffar Market workshop.
 Do you repair iPhone screens and will TrueTone work after replacement? 
 Yes, we offer display and glass replacement for all supported iPhone models. We program and transfer display serials so that features like TrueTone and touch sensitivity continue to function smoothly . Same-day replacement is available in 60 to 90 minutes.
 How long does battery replacement take, and is there a warranty? 
 iPhone battery replacements typically take 45 to 60 minutes . We use tested, high-cycle batteries that come with a dedicated warranty. Battery calibration and health optimization are done before final handover.
 Is my data safe during repair? Do I need to provide my passcode? 
 100% safe. For routine hardware repairs (screen, back glass, battery, charging port), we do not ask for your personal phone passcode. Your photos, messages, and personal files remain completely private and intact.
 Do you handle chip-level and motherboard repairs for MacBook? 
 Yes. In addition to display and battery replacements, our workshop specializes in micro-soldering and logic board repairs for MacBooks facing water damage, short circuits, or no-power symptoms.
 Can you fix broken Apple Watch glass or low audio in AirPods? 
 Yes. If your Apple Watch display is working and only the top glass is cracked, we can replace the outer glass to save costs. For AirPods, we handle single-side audio drop, battery drain, and charging case repairs.
