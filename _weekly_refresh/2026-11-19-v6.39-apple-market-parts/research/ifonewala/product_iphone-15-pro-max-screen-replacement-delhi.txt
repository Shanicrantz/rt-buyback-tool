 LCD Warranty – Terms & Conditions
 The following warranty conditions apply to the Budget LCD replacement display. Please read all terms carefully before purchasing or installing the product.
 01. Unboxing Video Mandatory 
 A clear, continuous unboxing video is mandatory for any warranty claim. The video should be recorded from before opening the package and must be without cuts or pauses.
 02. Flex Must Not Be Tampered With 
 Flex cable and display connector should not be tampered with, cut, damaged or replaced. Any soldering or modification will void the warranty.
 03. Only Touch Warranty 
 This product is covered under touch functionality warranty only. Issues related to display quality, brightness, spots or colour are not covered.
 04. Both-Side Courier Charges 
 Both-side courier charges will be borne by the customer for warranty claims. The customer can arrange the courier independently.
 05. No Warranty After Installation 
 Once the product is installed, no warranty claim will be accepted. Please test the product before installation.
 06. Same Condition 
 Product must be returned in the same condition as received, including original packaging, stickers and all parts, for warranty to be valid.
 07. Water or Physical Damage Not Accepted 
 Any water damage, physical damage, cracks, burn marks or mishandling will not be covered under warranty.
