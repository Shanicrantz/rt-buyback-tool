 Common Audio Symptoms: When Do You Need Speaker Repair? 
 Modern smartphones use spatial sound and stereo acoustic modules. Over time, pocket lint, moisture, and dust settle inside the micro-mesh. Here are common signs your device requires technical servicing:
 🔇 
 Low In-Call Volume 
 Earpiece volume is faint even at 100% level. 
 🔊 
 Crackling / Buzzing 
 Distorted audio on high frequencies or calls. 
 💧 
 Water Damage Static 
 Muffled metallic buzz after liquid exposure. 
 🚫 
 Zero Ringtone / Media 
 Blown voice coil or audio IC failure. 
 Ear Speaker vs Bottom Loudspeaker: What's the Difference? 
 👂 Earpiece Module (Top Frame) 
 Positioned on the top bezel strictly for voice calls and private audio. In modern iPhones, it is integrated alongside the proximity sensor and Face ID ribbon. Our technicians service the earpiece with high precision so Face ID stays 100% functional.
 📢 Loudspeaker / Ringer Buzzer (Bottom Frame) 
 Located near the charging port and primary microphone. It powers ringtones, speakerphone calls, video playback, and music. If the coil is shorted due to liquid or physical drops, a direct modular replacement restores factory sound.
