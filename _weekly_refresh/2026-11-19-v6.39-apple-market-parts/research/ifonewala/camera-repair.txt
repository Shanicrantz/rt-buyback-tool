 Camera Repair Services 
 Camera Problem? Start With a Proper Diagnosis. 
 Different camera faults require different repair solutions. We inspect the device first and explain the repair option before proceeding. 
 📷 
 Camera Not Turning On 
 Power-related faults, charging issues and internal component problems can be inspected to identify the actual cause. 
 🎯 
 Autofocus Problems 
 Focus hunting, inaccurate autofocus or a lens that struggles to lock focus can be checked during diagnosis. 
 🔭 
 Lens & Zoom Issues 
 Stuck zoom, unusual lens movement, grinding sounds or lens errors can be inspected for mechanical or electronic faults. 
 🖥️ 
 Display / LCD Problems 
 Cracked displays, black screens, flickering or touch/display faults can be assessed for suitable replacement or repair. 
 ⚡ 
 Battery & Power Faults 
 Fast battery drain, charging problems, unexpected shutdowns and power instability can be checked. 
 💧 
 Liquid / Moisture Damage 
 Moisture exposure can affect internal electronics. The device can be opened and inspected to assess the damage. 
 Common Problems 
 Is Your Camera Experiencing Any of These Problems? 
 ❌ Camera completely dead No power, no response or sudden shutdown. 
 ⚠️ Lens error Lens does not extend, retract or zoom correctly. 
 🎯 Focus not working Autofocus hunts, fails to lock or produces blurry images. 
 🖼️ Lines / black display LCD or display shows lines, spots, flicker or no image. 
 🔘 Buttons not responding Shutter, mode dial or control buttons stop working. 
 💾 Memory card error Camera fails to read, write or recognise a card. 
 🔋 Battery / charging issue Battery drains quickly or charging is inconsistent. 
 💧 Water / liquid damage Camera has been exposed to moisture or liquid.

 Frequently Asked Questions 
 Camera Repair FAQs 
 How much does camera repair cost? 
 The exact price depends on the camera model, fault and required component. The applicable estimate can be confirmed after physical inspection. 
 Can camera repair be completed the same day? 
 Repair time depends on the fault and parts availability. Simple issues may be completed faster, while complex internal repairs may require additional time. 
 Can a lens problem be repaired? 
 The solution depends on the type of lens damage. A repair or replacement option can be explained after diagnosis. 
 Can a water-damaged camera be repaired? 
 Repair success after liquid damage depends on the extent of internal damage and corrosion. Early inspection is recommended. 
 Will I receive an estimate before repair? 
 The applicable repair option and estimate should be discussed before repair is approved.
