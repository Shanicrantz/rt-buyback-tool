 iPhone 14 Pro Max Parts – FAQs 
 Answers to common questions about replacement parts. 
 Where can I buy iPhone 14 Pro Max parts online? 
 You can explore iPhone 14 Pro Max replacement parts through Ifonewala's dedicated display, battery, and body categories. Visit the product page to check pricing, specs, and stock availability. 
 Which display options are available for iPhone 14 Pro Max? 
 Display options may include Pulled Original, Soft OLED, and GX OLED, subject to stock. Check the dedicated display page for specs and feature support (like 120Hz ProMotion and True Tone). 
 How do I choose a compatible iPhone 14 Pro Max battery? 
 Verify your exact device model identifier, ribbon connector type, and battery capacity ratings listed on our product page. 
 What is included in iPhone 14 Pro Max body parts? 
 Body components include rear back glass panels, full chassis housings, volume/power button flexes, and camera lens rings. 
 Are iPhone 14 Pro Max and iPhone 14 Pro parts interchangeable? 
 No. They have entirely different physical chassis sizes, display panel sizes (6.7" vs 6.1"), and battery capacities. Always select parts made specifically for the 14 Pro Max.
